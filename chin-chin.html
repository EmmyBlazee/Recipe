Crunchy Chin Chin

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 20g of Flour
  • Sugar
  • Butter
  • Baking Powder
  • Eggs
  • Nutmeg (optional)
  • Milk
  • Vegetable Oil (for frying)
  • Water

Instructions

Preparation of the Dough

  1. Add flour, baking powder, butter, nutmeg, and sugar to a bowl. Mix until it resembles breadcrumbs
  2. Crack eggs into a separate bowl, whisk them, and add to the dry mixture
  3. Add a little water gradually until the dough is slightly stretchy but not sticky. Avoid adding too much water

Cutting and Frying

  1. Cut the dough into smaller chunks. Roll out each chunk on a flat surface and cut into desired shapes
  2. Pour oil into a pan and allow it to preheat until hot
  3. Fry the dough pieces until golden brown
  4. Remove from oil and allow to cool before serving

Coconut Rice

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1/2 teaspoon coconut oil (or vegetable oil)
  • 2 cups Thai jasmine white rice, rinsed well
  • 1/2 cup of canned coconut milk
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 3/4 cups water

Instructions

Making Coconut Rice

  1. Rub the oil over the bottom of a deep-sided pot.
  2. Place the rinsed rice in the pot along with the coconut milk, salt, and water. Set over medium-high heat and bring to a boil, stirring occasionally to prevent sticking.
  3. Once the liquid begins to bubble gently, stop stirring and reduce the heat to low to maintain a constant simmer.
  4. Cover the pot tightly with a lid and let it simmer for 15 to 20 minutes, or until most of the liquid has been absorbed by the rice.
  5. Check for doneness by pulling the rice aside with a fork. If a good amount of liquid remains, let it steam for a few more minutes. If the liquid is gone, turn off the heat.
  6. With the heat off, leave the covered pot on the hot burner for another 5 to 10 minutes to allow it to steam perfectly before serving.

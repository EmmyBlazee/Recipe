Fish Pepper Soup

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2kg Fresh Fish (Catfish or Tilapia)
  • 1 Medium Onion (chopped)
  • 2 Scotch Bonnet Peppers (Atarodo)
  • 1 tablespoon Ground Crayfish
  • 2 Ehuru (Calabash Nutmeg), toasted and ground
  • 2 sticks Uda (Negro Pepper)
  • 1/2 teaspoon Uziza seeds (ground)
  • Utazi Leaves (shredded)
  • 2 Seasoning Cubes
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

Preparation

  1. Wash the fish slices thoroughly with hot water to remove slime (if using catfish). Rinse with cold water
  2. Blend the scotch bonnet peppers, onions, and ginger (optional) together
  3. Toast the Ehuru seeds and grind them. Crush the Uda sticks slightly to release flavor

Cooking

  1. Place the fish in a pot and add enough water to just cover it. Set on medium heat
  2. Add the blended pepper mix, chopped onions, seasoning cubes, crayfish, and salt
  3. Bring to a boil, then add the ground Ehuru, Uziza, and Uda. Gently swirl the pot to mix (avoid stirring vigorously to keep fish intact)
  4. Cook for 5-8 minutes until the fish is tender
  5. Add the shredded Utazi leaves for a bitter-sweet flavor note. Simmer for another 2 minutes
  6. Taste and adjust seasoning if needed. Serve hot

Fried Rice

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 3 cups of cold leftover cooked rice
  • 1/2 - 1 cup of oleo (or butter)
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 1 cup of frozen peas
  • 3 eggs, beaten
  • 1/2 cup of soy sauce (to taste)

Instructions

Preparing Fried Rice

  1. Melt oleo in a large non-stick skillet.
  2. Add onion and carrots. Sauté until onions are transparent and carrots are crisp-tender, about 5 minutes.
  3. Add peas and cook for one more minute until thawed.
  4. Push the vegetables to one side of the pan.
  5. Scramble the eggs in the empty side of the pan.
  6. Mix the eggs into the vegetables and season with pepper.
  7. Add a small amount of rice, followed by the soy sauce.
  8. Slowly mix in the remaining rice by the spoonful until all ingredients are well blended.
  9. Fry until steamy hot, stirring often.

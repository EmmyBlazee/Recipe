Homemade Ice Cream

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 tin Condensed Milk
  • 1 cup Heavy Whipping Cream
  • 2 teaspoons Vanilla Extract
  • 3/4 cup White Sugar
  • 6 large Egg Yolks

Instructions

Preparing the Custard Base

  1. Stir milk, sugar, and cream together in a pan over low heat until sugar completely dissolves (about 5 minutes). Remove pan from heat
  2. In a separate bowl, whisk the egg yolks
  3. Whisking constantly, slowly pour about a third of the hot cream mixture into the yolks (tempering)
  4. Whisk the yolk mixture back into the pot with the remaining cream

Cooking and Chilling

  1. Return pan to medium-low heat and gently cook, stirring constantly, until mixture is thick enough to coat the back of a spoon (about 170°F)
  2. Strain through a fine-mesh sieve into a bowl
  3. Cool mixture to room temperature, then cover and chill for at least 4 hours or overnight
  4. Store the ice cream in the freezer until needed

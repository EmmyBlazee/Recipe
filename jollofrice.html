Jollof Rice

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups Precooked long grain rice
  • 5 Tablespoons tomato paste
  • 4 whole tomatoes
  • 1 red bell pepper
  • 2 scotch bonnet peppers
  • 100ml vegetable oil
  • About 600ml Meat or Chicken stock
  • A small onion (sliced)
  • 1 tablespoon ground crayfish (optional)
  • ½ teaspoon each of Thyme and curry
  • 2 small Bay leaves (optional)
  • 1 teaspoon salt to taste
  • 1 stock cube

Instructions

Preparing Jollof Rice

  1. First, prepare a little stew or sauce by blending the fresh tomatoes and peppers together; then dry out excess liquid by boiling in a pot for few minutes
  2. After boiling it, place a pot on heat, add some oil, add chopped onions, fry a bit and then add the blended tomatoes & pepper mix, fry for about 10 minutes
  3. Then add the parboiled rice and mix thoroughly; the liquid in the pot should be at the same level as the rice in the pot. Cover the pot and leave the rice to cook on low to medium heat (High heat will burn the rice before it is well cooked)
  4. When the liquid in the rice is almost dried, add the ground crayfish then cover the pot and simmer until there is no more liquid in the rice
  5. Mix thoroughly and your Nigerian Jollof Rice is ready to be served

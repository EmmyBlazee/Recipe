Lemonade

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 4-6 lemons
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 4 cups water
  • Ice cubes

Instructions

Making Lemonade

  1. Squeeze the juice from 4-6 lemons into a large bowl or pitcher.
  2. Add 1 cup of sugar to the lemon juice and stir until dissolved.
  3. Add 4 cups of water and stir well to combine.
  4. Taste and adjust sweetness or tartness as needed.
  5. Serve over ice cubes in glasses.

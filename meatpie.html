Meatpie

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 4 cups of all-purpose Flour
  • Baking Powder
  • 200g of Butter
  • Blended Meat
  • Diced Carrot
  • Onions
  • Salt
  • Seasoning (Thyme & Pepper)
  • Vegetable oil
  • Thyme

Instructions

Preparation of the Filling

  1. Add oil in a frying pan
  2. Add onion to the oil, cook and stir for 3 minutes
  3. Add carrots, cook and stir for 3 minutes
  4. Add seasoning and salt to the mix
  5. Add cubed potatoes and stir. Pour water and allow it to cook until it thickens

Preparation of the Dough

  1. Add Flour, Sugar, Salt, Butter and Baking Powder into a large bowl
  2. Mix the above content until you get a crumbly texture
  3. Beat two eggs with cold water and add it to the flour mixture and mix it until a dough is formed
  4. Wrap the dough in the bowl with a cling film and allow it rest for 30 minutes

Baking the Pastry

  1. Preheat oven to 175°C
  2. Roll out the dough and cut out the desired shape. Combine all leftover dough and roll it again to repeat the same previous process
  3. Place two tablespoons of the earlier prepared filling in the center, and fold over the dough. Seal the edges with a fork
  4. Place the Pastry on a tray and put in the oven and allow it to bake for 25-30 minutes until golden
  5. Serve after it cools down

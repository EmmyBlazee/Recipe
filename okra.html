Okra Soup

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • Sliced Okra (medium bowl)
  • 1kg Meat (Chicken/Beef)
  • 500g Mackerel Fish
  • Stockfish Head
  • 5 Shombo Peppers
  • 2 Scotch Bonnet Peppers
  • 1/2 cup Ground Crayfish
  • 200ml Palm Oil
  • 2 Seasoning Cubes
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

Preparation

  1. Slice the okra and set aside. Remove seeds from shombo peppers, combine with scotch bonnet and blend/pound. Grind the crayfish
  2. Wash the fish and meat thoroughly

Cooking the Stock

  1. Season the meat/fish with salt, seasoning cubes, sliced onions, and a cup of water. Boil for 10-15 minutes. Remove the meat/fish, leaving the stock in the pot
  2. Add the stockfish and more water to the pot. Cook for about 10 minutes until soft

Making the Soup

  1. Add palm oil, crayfish, blended peppers, and salt. Boil for 8 minutes
  2. Return the cooked meat/fish to the pot and simmer for 3 minutes
  3. Add the sliced okra and stir. Add the mackerel fish on top
  4. Simmer for 3-4 minutes. Serve with your choice of swallow (Eba, Fufu, Pounded Yam)

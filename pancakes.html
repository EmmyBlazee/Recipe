Fluffy Pancakes

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 3 cups of Flour
  • 5 tablespoons of Sugar
  • 3 tablespoons of Powdered Milk
  • 2 Eggs
  • 1 teaspoon of dry Pepper
  • Vegetable oil
  • Water

Instructions

Preparation of the Batter

  1. Add the flour, sugar, and pepper into a mixing bowl
  2. Whisk the eggs with the milk in a separate bowl
  3. Mix the dry ingredients with the egg mixture
  4. Add water gradually until the mixture becomes semi-liquid. Allow it to rest for 2 minutes

Cooking the Pancakes

  1. Heat a frying pan for a minute
  2. Add 1 teaspoon of oil to the pan and spread it around with a brush
  3. Pour an appropriate amount of the batter into the pan
  4. When bubbles start to form on top and the edges look dry, flip the pancake to the other side
  5. Wait for about two minutes, then remove the pancake from the pan
  6. Repeat the process until the batter is finished

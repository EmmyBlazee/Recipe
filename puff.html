Puff-Puff

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 600g All-purpose Flour
  • 150g Sugar
  • 10g Yeast
  • 1/2 teaspoon Nutmeg
  • Pinch of Salt
  • Warm Water
  • Vegetable Oil (for deep frying)

Instructions

Making the Batter

  1. In a large bowl, mix the flour, sugar, yeast, nutmeg, and salt together
  2. Gradually add warm water while mixing until you get a smooth batter that is not too watery
  3. Cover the bowl with aluminum foil or a clean cloth and let it rise for about 45 minutes

Frying

  1. Heat a generous amount of vegetable oil in a deep pan or pot
  2. Test the oil temperature by dropping a small amount of batter; if it floats immediately, the oil is ready
  3. Using your hand, scoop a little batter and squeeze it through your thumb and index finger to form a ball, dropping it into the hot oil
  4. Fry until golden brown, flipping to ensure even cooking
  5. Remove from oil and drain on paper towels before serving

Spring Rolls

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of Flour
  • Blended Meat
  • Two Seasoning Cubes
  • Diced Cabbage
  • Diced Onion
  • Diced Pepper
  • 1 teaspoon Curry powder
  • Carrot
  • 1/2 teaspoon of Salt
  • Oil

Instructions

Preparing the Filling

  1. Cook it on low heat
  2. Preheat two tablespoons of oil in a pan
  3. Add Pepper, salt, cube & curry powder to the filling
  4. Add the blended beef to the filling
  5. Add shredded cabbage and carrots
  6. Stir the filling and cook for 5 minutes

Preparing the Spring Rolls Sheet

  1. Add flour in a Bowl
  2. Add water in the bowl
  3. Mix the above together to make the paste
  4. Roll the dough into thin sheets and cut into desired shape
  5. Re-roll the leftover dough and repeat the above procedure

Frying the Spring Rolls

  1. Add two teaspoons of the filling to the springrolls sheet
  2. Preheat the oil in the pan
  3. Fry the springrolls till it turns golden
  4. Serve after it cools down

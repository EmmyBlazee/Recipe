Classic Tomato Stew

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • Tomato Paste or Fresh Tomatoes
  • 2 Large Onions
  • 1 Large Red Bell Pepper (Tatashe)
  • 2 Scotch Bonnet Peppers (Atarodo)
  • 1/2 tablespoon Dry Thyme
  • 1 teaspoon Curry Powder
  • 2 Seasoning Cubes
  • Salt to taste
  • 1/2 - 1 cup Vegetable Oil
  • Meat Stock
  • Assorted Meat (Chicken, Beef, or Fish)

Instructions

Preparation

  1. Wash, chop, and blend the tomatoes, bell peppers, scotch bonnet peppers, garlic, ginger, and onions. Blend in small batches for a smooth consistency

Cooking the Stew

  1. Pour the blended tomato mixture into a pot and cook until the water dries up
  2. Add vegetable oil and fry the tomato paste for a few minutes
  3. Add the meat stock, cooked meat pieces, seasoning cubes, curry, thyme, and salt
  4. Cook on low-medium heat, stirring occasionally, until the oil completely separates from the tomato blend (about 15-20 minutes)
  5. Taste to ensure the raw tomato flavor is gone, then turn off the heat
  6. Serve with Rice, Yam, or Bread

Vegetable Soup

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 bunch Ugu (Fluted Pumpkin) leaves
  • 3 bunches Waterleaf
  • 2kg Goat Meat (Roasted/Smoked preferred)
  • 1 cup Ground Crayfish
  • 1.5 cups Palm Oil
  • 1 Medium Onion
  • 5 Large Tomatoes
  • 1 teaspoon Pepper (or to taste)
  • 2 Seasoning Cubes
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

Preparation

  1. Wash and chop the vegetables (Ugu and Waterleaf) separately. Set aside
  2. Wash and prepare the goat meat

Cooking

  1. Heat the palm oil in a pot. Add chopped onions and tomatoes. Stir-fry until onions are translucent
  2. Add the goat meat, pepper, and seasoning cubes. Cook for a few minutes to let flavors absorb
  3. Add the washed waterleaf first, stir well, and cook for about 2-3 minutes until it wilts
  4. Add the Ugu leaves and ground crayfish. Stir thoroughly to combine
  5. Cover and simmer for just 1-2 minutes. Do not overcook the vegetables to retain nutrients
  6. Taste for salt and adjust if necessary. Turn off the heat
  7. Serve hot with Fufu, Eba, Pounded Yam, or Rice
